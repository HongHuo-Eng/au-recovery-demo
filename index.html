    How to use this presentation

    Instructions

    Explore real observations, compare local models, and draft experiments to test their suggestions. Recommendations are planning aids, not validated protocols. The Materials and Process cases describe separate studies.

    Quick start

    1. Start with the observations. Open Materials or Process, choose a Dataset, then select Reported observations only under Prediction method.
    2. Compare predictions. Try Gaussian process and the simple baselines. Check the plotted response and the whole-condition holdout table before considering a candidate.
    3. Set a decision target. Adjust the Materials percentage or Process concentration limits. Compare the suggestion with measured settings and try a more conservative screening rule.
    4. Plan a test. Use Add to validation plan when available. Review the draft and its missing conditions; use Evidence & methods for the scientific limits.
    Materials: what does the target percentage mean?

    Retain at least sets a fraction of the highest observed uptake in this case. At 95%, the target is 0.95 times that value. It does not mean 95% gold recovery.

    The browser selects the lowest CS:GO ratio meeting the target under the chosen method and screening rule, within the displayed range. Ratio describes composition; zero is pure GO. A lower ratio is not automatically cheaper or a smaller treatment dose.

    Gaussian process + model band supplies a mean and model standard deviation. PCHIP interpolation is a shape-preserving interpolator; Linear regression baseline and Training-mean baseline provide simple comparisons. Reported observations only restricts selection to measured settings.

    Screening conservatism: what changes when I select 1 or 1.96?

    For Materials, screening uses predicted mean minus the selected multiple of model SD. For Process, it requires Au mean plus that multiple to stay below the Au maximum, and Cu mean minus it to stay above the Cu minimum.

    The plotted GP band remains mean ±1.96 × model SD; this control changes screening, not the band or training. These are assumption-dependent rules, not calibrated confidence guarantees. Baselines do not inherit GP uncertainty, so this control is disabled for them.

    Process: concentration limits, unconfirmed labels and wavelengths

    Maximum reported Au concentration favours less Au remaining in solution. Minimum reported Cu concentration (mg/L) favours retaining reported Cu in solution. Check each displayed unit. These limits do not measure product purity or establish selective recovery.

    The unconfirmed-label case starts in observation-only mode. Temporarily interpret labels as dosage enables a conditional continuous scenario; it does not confirm the labels' meaning or make their units mg. This case does not enable Add to validation plan.

    Au measurement channel changes the Au observations, predictions and validation metrics together. Four wavelengths describe the same samples, not four independent experiments. The displayed channel is not established as the best analytical channel.

    Reading plots, error bars and “No supported candidate”

    Markers show reported observations; curves show model estimates; the diamond identifies the selected condition. A model estimate at a previously measured input is still a prediction. Source error bars have unspecified statistics and are different from the GP band.

    In Au–Cu trade-off, left means lower Au and up means higher Cu. Pareto candidates represent trade-offs among the displayed objectives, not proven superior treatments.

    No supported candidate means no evaluated point passes the current constraints and screening rule within the range. It is a valid result, not proof that the chemistry is impossible. Review justified targets or plan additional measurements.

    Validation: how should I interpret MAE and RMSE?

    MAE is the average absolute prediction error. RMSE gives larger errors more weight. Lower values are preferable within the same case, response and units; do not compare their magnitudes across different cases.

    Whole-condition holdout leaves out one complete setting and refits using the others. With only three or five settings, each fold trains on two or four. Holding out an endpoint requires extrapolation beyond that fold's training range. Neither a low error nor the best baseline score establishes reliable new-batch performance.

    Models were trained offline on CPU. Browser controls select saved prediction grids and filter candidates; they do not retrain models or create new experimental evidence.

    Validation plan: combinations, controls and session state

    Candidate memo records selections. Include memo candidates in factor levels adds eligible selections to the matrix. Default levels give 3 ratios × 3 doses = 9 combinations. Three independent treatment blocks give 27 treatments, plus one no-adsorbent control per block: 30 planned treatments.

    Generate experiment draft uses a fixed seed to randomise order within each block. Repeated instrument readings cannot replace independent treatments. Missing batch, preparation, volume, pH, time or temperature information keeps the plan incomplete; completing the fields does not establish feasibility.

    Every row remains proposed and unexecuted, with no fabricated outcome. Combining factor levels does not combine the two models into a joint performance prediction. Selections and the memo live in the current page session; refreshing resets them. This presentation offers no plan export.

    Evidence limits and access to displayed values

    Observed means reported experimental values; Predicted means model outputs; Proposed means tests not performed. Matched measured initial concentrations and other conditions are incomplete. Do not infer an initial concentration from a sample label. Reduced liquid-phase Au does not establish metallic-gold yield.

    Original attachments and data downloads are not offered here. This is not confidentiality protection: values needed to render the charts reach the browser and can be inspected or saved. Removing the page later cannot revoke existing copies.

    Measurements, predictions and proposed experiments are labelled separately. This demo does not establish generalization across batches or improved metallic-gold recovery.
